Keep it healthy

Best practices & common mistakes

Do

  • Keep one ORCID iD for your entire career.
  • Add a personal backup email to both accounts.
  • Review Scholar's article updates by email.
  • Make both profiles public and keep affiliations current.
  • Check your profiles every few months.

Don't

  • Create duplicate ORCID records. Ask ORCID support to merge them.
  • Let "Apply updates automatically" add other people's papers.
  • Rely only on a work email you may lose.
  • Add predatory-journal or non-scholarly items to inflate metrics.
  • Share your password or sign up through third-party sites.
