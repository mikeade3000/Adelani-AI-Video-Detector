    📄Reports

    Generate promotion-exercise reports from the current submitted data. Each report can be previewed, then downloaded as a Word document. Figures are computed live from the records in the system.

    1 · Overall Summary Report

    Cycle summary (January vs July notional) and a breakdown by promotion grade level, with recommended totals.

    2 · Promotion Defence Report (by MDA)

    Per-MDA listing of total officers presented, grouped and totalled, in the promotion-defence layout.

    3 · Promotion Exercise Report (RSTP narrative)

    Narrative report with an overall criteria-met summary and a grade-bracket performance table (GL06–10 / GL12–16).
