    02Print Eligibility Slips — Verified and Authentic Data

    Every officer on the uploaded eligibility lists is stored here, with the list's details (including the Eligibility/SEAP Number) and, where they submitted one, their Form A details and photograph. Administrators can correct an officer's slip details or photograph with ✎ Edit, and use Fix MDA grouping to move officers uploaded earlier to the MDA on their list.     🔗MDA Print Links

    Each MDA in Verify & Print Slips can have one private link. Type the MDA's email address, click Create link, then send it with Email link (it opens your own Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook or email program with the message ready). Whoever opens the link must type that email address — without it the link shows nothing, and 5 wrong addresses lock the link for 15 minutes. It opens only that MDA's slips for 1 hour: print, download as PDF, or download the list in Excel — nothing else. New link replaces a link (the old one stops working at once); Switch off blocks it. Every sign-in, print and download is recorded in the log.
